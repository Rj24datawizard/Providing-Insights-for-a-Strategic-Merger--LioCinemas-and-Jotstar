**1. Business problem statement**

**Company :** LioCinema and Jotstar

**Problem :** Both the companies decide for a strategic merger but before combining the platforms, management needs to understand how each platform performs and how its users behave.

**Why is Data Analysis Needed:** Data Analysis is needed to understand their individual performance based on which we can combine their strength to create a stronger OTT business

****
🎯 **Project objectives**  

- Compare the content libraries and platform characteristics.
- Analyse subscriber growth and demographic patterns.
- Identify inactivity trends across customer segments.
- Examine subscription upgrade and downgrade behaviour.
- Evaluate content consumption and device preferences.
- Develop an interactive comparison dashboard for stakeholders.
- Translate analytical findings into strategic recommendations.
